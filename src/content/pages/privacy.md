# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 24, 2026*

Fieldnotes is an independent personal publication. Your privacy is respected by default.

## 1. No Tracking & No Invasive Cookies

- Fieldnotes does not use third-party behavioral advertising cookies, fingerprinting scripts, or cross-site tracking.
- The site is statically hosted via **GitHub Pages**, which serves pre-rendered HTML, CSS, and images directly from global edge servers.

## 2. Server Logs

When you request pages on this website, standard technical web server logs (including IP address, browser user-agent, and requested file path) may be temporarily processed by GitHub infrastructure solely for security, network diagnostics, and DDoS mitigation as outlined in the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement).

## 3. Local Storage

This site stores only one functional preference in your browser's local storage:
- **`theme`**: Remembers your preferred reading mode (`light` or `dark`). This data never leaves your browser.

## 4. Contact & Communications

If you contact the author directly via email, your email address and message content are used solely to reply to your inquiry and are never sold or shared.
